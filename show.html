Wow moments

Famous experiments that make a hall gasp

Well known science, big on stage. We pick the mix with your school.

Foam

Elephant toothpaste

Hydrogen peroxide, yeast and soap make a huge column of warm foam, because the yeast speeds up the breakdown of the peroxide into water and oxygen gas.

Adults only

Flame colors

Different metal salts turn a flame different colors. It is the same science behind fireworks.

Adults only

Dry ice vapor

Dry ice is solid carbon dioxide, far colder than a freezer. In water it makes thick white fog that rolls across the stage.

Geyser

Soda and mints

Dropping mints into a bottle of fizzy drink makes the gas escape all at once and shoot up in a fountain.

Color

Red cabbage rainbow

Red cabbage juice changes color with acids and bases, from red and pink through purple to blue and green.

Air

Balloon in a flask

Heat and cooling change the pressure inside a flask, and a balloon shows it. The big screen shows what the gas particles are doing.

Safety note. Fire and dry ice are for trained adults only. Please do not try them at home. Ask us about the show and we will plan it around your school's rules.

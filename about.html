About Fabula Spark

Fabula Spark is an educational brand. Our live science show brings experiments and stories to school assemblies, so students see how discoveries happen.

Questions schools ask

Short answers. Ask us anything else on WhatsApp.

Which ages do you work with?

Tell us your group and we shape the show to fit it.

Where do the shows take place?

We bring the show to you. Tell us your location and we will confirm.

Can the show include a story?

Yes. We wrap experiments in a story that explains how the discovery happened.

Do you only do science shows?

Science shows are our focus today. More Spark experiences are on the way.

How do we book?

Send us a message with your school, age group and preferred dates.

Is the show suitable for young students?

We shape the show to the age group you tell us about, from kindergarten to older students.

Can we set rules for the show?

Yes. Tell us about your school's rules and your hall, and we plan the show around them.

What language is the show in?

Arabic or English. Tell us what your students need.

What does it cost, and how long is it?

It depends on your school, hall and group. We send the details after you message us, before anything is confirmed.

Where have you performed so far?

Around three shows with the General Authority for Cultural Palaces, including one large show. Two shows at private venues, and one show at SCLS, a national school.

Can I share this with my team?

Yes. Use the share buttons at the bottom of any page to send it on WhatsApp or copy the link.
